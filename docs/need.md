## Human Need: Campus Navigation at UW

### Problem Statement
The UW campus is over 700 acres with many buildings spread out over a complex layout. New students (freshmen, transfer and international students) have difficulty navigating campus which leads to late arrivals, increased stress and time wasted between classes. Google Maps and the like are helpful when navigating in general, but they don’t have the kind of specific information you need on a college campus, such as integration with class schedules or building-specific details.

## Evidence: Student Interviews

We interviewed 4 UW students during Spring Quarter 2026, targeting students across different years to understand the range of navigation challenges on campus.

---

**Participant 1** — 1st Year, Computer Science

- Has been a UW student for 1 year
- Was late to class on the first day of section this quarter because the section was in an unfamiliar hall and took a long time to find the room number
- Currently uses Google Maps and the UW campus map to find buildings
- Main frustration: keycard access to buildings when locked
- Does not reliably know building hours (finds out by trial and error or Googling)
- Has not missed a bus due to navigation (relies on Google Maps for walking time)
- Said the app would be **useful but mainly for the first few days** of class since routes are memorized quickly afterward
- Wished the app had **interior building maps** for finding specific rooms

---

**Participant 2** - 1st Year, Computer Science

- Has been a UW student for 1 year
- Has been a few minutes late to class due to navigation, though it did not significantly impact learning
- Uses Google Maps to get to the building, then guesses their way around inside
- Main frustration: crowded sidewalks with slow foot traffic between classes
- Does not know building hours outside of the standard 9 AM–5 PM window
- Has missed a bus due to poor timing but was only a couple minutes late to class as a result
- Said they **would not find the app personally useful** since Google Maps handles the first 1-2 trips and then the route is memorized
- Wished for **better building directories** showing room numbering systems so students don't have to wander inside buildings

---

**Participant 3** - 3rd Year, Informatics

- Has been a UW student for 3 years
- Has not been late due to navigation (knows campus layout well)
- Occasionally uses Google Maps for buildings they haven't visited
- Does not track building hours and mostly visits during standard business hours
- Has not missed a bus due to navigation
- Said the app would be **more useful for freshmen and transfers** but appreciated the "Route All Classes" feature for optimizing paths between back-to-back classes even for upperclassmen
- Wished for **interior navigation** to find specific rooms in large buildings

---

**Participant 4** - Transfer Student, 4th Year, Civil Engineering

- Has been a UW student for 1.5 years
- Was late during their **first week as a transfer** (couldn't find the Engineering buildings on the north side of campus)
- Uses Google Maps to reach a building then wanders inside until finding the room
- Has had to rush between classes on opposite ends of campus (e.g. Odegaard to ECE Building)
- Does not know building hours and assumes standard business hours
- Said the app **would have been very useful during their first quarter** as a transfer student
- Called the "Route All Classes" feature the **standout feature** for helping new students plan efficient paths between buildings
- Wished the app indicated **which entrance to use** for each building

---

## Key Findings

- 3 out of 4 participants have been late to class or missed a bus due to navigation issues
- All 4 participants rely on Google Maps as their primary navigation tool but note it does not help inside buildings or with schedule planning
- Interior building navigation (finding specific rooms) was mentioned by 3 out of 4 participants as the most wanted missing feature
- The app is most valuable for **new students** (freshmen and transfers) with utility decreasing as students memorize routes over time
- Class schedule integration with routing (the "Route All Classes" feature) was highlighted as a unique value-add not available in any current tool

## Current Solutions & Why They Are Not Working

**Google Maps:**
- Shows building locations and walking times
- Does NOT show room numbers, floor plans, or specific entrances
- Does NOT show building hours or accessibility information
- Does NOT integrate with a student's class schedule

**UW Interactive Map (maps.uw.edu):**
- Has building names and some general info
- Not designed for navigation (no walking directions)
- Not optimized for mobile use
- No class schedule integration

**Gap:** No single tool combines GPS walking navigation, building-specific information (hours, accessibility, elevators), and class schedule integration in a mobile-friendly app built specifically for the UW campus.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** First-year undergraduates
- **Secondary:** Transfer students
- **Tertiary:** International students

## Our Solution

UW Navigator addresses these needs by combining GPS-based campus navigation, building details (hours, accessibility, elevator access), and class schedule integration into a single mobile app. The "Route All Classes" feature in particular directly addresses the pain point of planning efficient paths between back-to-back classes which is something that no existing tool provides.
